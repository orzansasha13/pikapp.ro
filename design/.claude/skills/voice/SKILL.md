# pikapp.ro voice (on-screen copy)

The line-up speaks for itself, and so does the gear. Understatement is the credibility.

## Rules

1. Romanian with full diacritics (ă â î ș ț). Scene and gear words stay in English as DJs say them: CDJ, booth, setup, gig, rider. Use the words in `glossary.md`.
2. Sentence case. "Alege data", not "Alege Data".
3. Buttons: a verb first, 1 to 3 words, plus the channel when it matters ("Rezervă pe WhatsApp"). The button says what happens.
4. Numbers and model names instead of adjectives. "2× CDJ-3000", not "decks de top".
5. No em dashes. Use commas, full stops or parentheses.
6. Never: "experiență unică", "pasionați de muzică", "echipa noastră de profesioniști", "premium", "de top", "nu rata".
7. No urgency and no exclamation marks. Real dates and real states only ("ocupat pe 10 oct").
8. Success and confirmation lines state the fact: "Mesaj pregătit în WhatsApp." No thanks, no congratulations.
9. Errors say what happened and how to fix it: "Alege o dată ca să vezi ce e liber."

## Out of scope

Instagram captions (lowercase, English) and emails follow the pikapp-voice skill, not this file.
