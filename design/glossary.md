# glossary.md

One word per concept, in the words our users say. The UI word is what appears on screen. The avoid column lists words we have used before and stop using.

| Concept | UI word (RO) | Meaning | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| A ready-made combination of gear for one night | setup | Fixed contents, fixed price, bookable by date | pachet, kit, bundle |
| Decks and mixer only, no speakers | booth | 2 players plus a mixer, cables included | "consolă DJ", "echipament profesional" |
| Speakers for the room | sunet | PA speakers and sub, Electro-Voice EKX | "sistem audio premium" |
| The price that already includes everything | tot inclus | Delivery, setup and TVA are inside the number shown | "de la", "preț estimativ" |
| Bringing and installing the gear | livrare și montaj | We deliver, set up, and collect after the event | "transport separat" |
| The two ways to book | setup standard / ofertă personalizată | Standard has a price on screen; personalizată gets a quote | "solicită ofertă" for everything |
| The DJ's technical requirements | rider | The list of gear an artist asks for | "specificații" |
| A single booked night of playing | gig | Used as the DJ says it | "eveniment" when talking to DJs |
| Gear already booked for that date | ocupat | Shown only when true | "stoc limitat", "ultimele bucăți" |
| A client who has booked before | client recurent | Internal word, never shown on screen | "VIP", "membru" |
