# day-one.md

What a new teammate must know about pikapp.ro before their first task.

## The venture

pikapp is a Bucharest underground electronic music brand run by three co-founders (Matei Prisecaru, Sasha Orzan, Vlad Costache) through BASS MVNT S.R.L. It has three verticals: events, DJ and sound equipment rental, and artist booking.

This folder covers **pikapp.ro**. Today pikapp.ro is a single page built in Framer and it is mainly the rental site: the page title is "Închirieri echipamente DJ", the hero says "Curated to elevate." and the main actions are "Solicită Ofertă" and a "Get Price on WhatsApp" button on every piece of gear. Rentals are the vertical that already makes money. Events and booking pages are separate briefs.

## Who it is for

1. **Repeat bookers.** Bucharest DJs and promoters who need a standard setup for one night. They know what a CDJ-3000 is and they want a price and a yes.
2. **Custom and ongoing clients.** Venues, corporate events, weddings. They need a combination that changes each time, often with sound for a room.

In our discovery interviews with six Bucharest promoters and DJs the split was 3 and 3: standard repeat bookers preferred visible prices, custom and ongoing clients preferred a quote.

How a repeat booker puts it (our paraphrase, not a transcript): "Am gig sâmbătă, îmi trebuie două CDJ-uri și un mixer. Cât e cu tot cu transport?"

## What they do today

They message us on WhatsApp, describe the gig, and wait for a price. Or they compare with Rentech, which shows lower numbers and then adds transport, setup and TVA separately, or with Gruuv, which runs everything through WhatsApp per product.

## The one job of pikapp.ro

Let a promoter book a standard setup for a date at a known, all-in price in under a minute. Send everyone else to a quote, without making them fill the same form.

## What success looks like

- A booking starts with a message that already contains the setup, the date and the price, instead of "Salutare! Mă interesează o ofertă de preț."
- Repeat bookers rebook with one message.

## Constraints

- Framer site, no backend. A booking is a prefilled WhatsApp message to +40 724 262 040 that a person confirms.
- Service area: Bucharest, Ploiești and nearby.
- Delivery is free over 300 RON and setup is included (already promised on the live site).
- Night prices sit at roughly 800, 1,800 and 2,800 RON per tier.
- UI language is Romanian with diacritics. Gear and scene words stay in English (CDJ, setup, booth, gig).
- Three founders, no paid acquisition to speak of. Most clients already know us from the scene and our events.

## Out of scope

Online payment, user accounts, the events and booking pages, Instagram content.

## What we are guessing

- Where bookings start. We have not measured it. Guess: most begin as a WhatsApp message from someone who already knows us, not from the site.
- The exact contents of each price tier. The screen copy lists our current proposal; Matei confirms it against the rental sheet.
