# never-list.md

Things pikapp.ro never does, whatever the brief says. If a request conflicts with a line here, stop and ask.

1. **Never show a number that grows later.** If transport, setup or TVA are not inside the price, they are written on the same line as the price, at the same size. "De la 800 RON" followed by extras on the next screen is the Rentech pattern we compete against.
2. **Never fake availability or urgency.** No "ultimul set disponibil", no countdowns, no "doar azi". "Ocupat" appears only when the gear is actually booked for that date. (This is the mechanic refused in `product-loop.md`.)
3. **Never ask for contact details before a standard price.** No email, phone or account before someone sees what a standard setup costs.
4. **Never message a client on our behalf.** Claude can prepare a WhatsApp link or a draft. A person taps send.
5. **Never use an asset without a source.** If it is not in `materials/LICENSES.md` with a licence, it does not go in a screen. No lorem ipsum in anything that leaves this folder.
6. **Never chase a closed booking.** After the gear is collected we send the invoice and stop. No re-engagement messages, points or streaks.
