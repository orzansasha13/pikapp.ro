# product-loop.md

The loop a pikapp.ro rental client lives in: trigger, action, reward, investment. Phases marked **GUESS** are not yet backed by anything we measured or heard.

## Trigger

**Today, honestly:** we message them, or they already know us. Clients come from the scene, our own events and word of mouth, then write to us on WhatsApp. The site is mostly where they check the gear list after someone sent them the link. **GUESS:** we have not measured where bookings start.

**Target by day 30:** an internal trigger. "Am gig sâmbătă și n-am decks" turns into opening pikapp.ro or our WhatsApp before searching anything else.

## Action

**Today:** open the site, scroll to the gear, tap "Get Price on WhatsApp" on one item, WhatsApp opens with "Salutare! Mă interesează o ofertă de preț.", explain the date, the gear and the place, wait for a person to reply with a price, confirm.

**Step removed:** asking for the price and waiting for the reply. Standard setups show the all-in price on the screen, and the WhatsApp message opens already written with the setup, the date and the price. The first message is a booking, not a question. This is what the `rezerva-setup` screen does.

## Reward

- **Expected part:** the gear arrives, gets set up and works, and the bill is the number they saw. This is reliability, and it should stay boring.
- **Variable part, tribe:** a renter who plays on pikapp gear can get tagged and reposted on our channels, and the ones who fit get considered for slots at pikapp events and for pikapp Booking. **GUESS:** we have not asked renters whether they want this.

## Investment

What the client puts in: their usual setup, their venue address and their rider stay in our WhatsApp thread and our client sheet. Next time, "aceeași configurație, sâmbătă 18" is enough, and we already know where the venue's loading door is. Their effort makes the next booking shorter. **GUESS:** we do not yet keep this consistently for every client.

## Stop condition

The loop ends when the gear is collected and the invoice is sent. Two messages are allowed outside a booking conversation: the delivery reminder the day before, and the invoice. Nothing after that.

## Ethics gate

**Facilitator.** We use the same gear at our own events and Matei plays on it as a DJ. The loop gives back time (no waiting for a price) and a number that does not grow. It stops when the client has what they came for.

## The mechanic we refuse

**Fake availability pressure:** "ultimul set disponibil sâmbătă", countdown timers, "doar azi". It would push a few bookings and break the one promise the screen makes, that what we show is true. "Ocupat" appears only when the gear is booked for that date. Also written in `never-list.md`, line 2.

## The sentence

In my product I will use a one-message booking with the all-in price already written, and I will never use fake availability pressure, because a client who books under a false "last set" will not trust our "tot inclus" price either.
